## 2. Provenance Standards & Invariants
- **LIVE:** Direct observation from approved on-corridor sensor or permitted real-time API.
- **REPLAY:** Historical recorded telemetry replayed chronologically.
- **SIMULATION:** Output of physics-based or behavioral simulator (e.g. SUMO).
- **PREDICTED:** Machine learning or algorithmic forecast output.
- **STALE:** Valid observation exceeding freshness threshold (>180s traffic, >900s energy).
- **INVALID:** Rejected by schema, bounds, or timestamp sanity validation.
